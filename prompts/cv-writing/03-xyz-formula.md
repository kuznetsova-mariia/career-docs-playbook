# The XYZ Formula

The achievement format recommended by Google recruiters:

**Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]**

The formula was popularized by Laszlo Bock, Google's former SVP of People
Operations, in *Work Rules!*. Every resume bullet should carry all three
parts: result → metric → method.

- **X — the result.** What you achieved (the outcome, not the task).
- **Y — the measure.** The number that proves it: %, $, time, scale, count.
- **Z — the method.** What you actually did to get there.

Example:
> Reduced checkout failures by 32% over one quarter by rewriting the payment
> retry logic and adding idempotency keys.

Here X = "reduced checkout failures", Y = "by 32% over one quarter",
Z = "by rewriting the payment retry logic and adding idempotency keys." A
bullet missing Y reads as an opinion; one missing Z reads as a claim you
can't back up.
