# Power Verbs

The goal: every sentence in your resume starts with an action verb.

## Not like this
- responsible for project delivery
- was involved in migration
- helped with GTM strategy

## Like this
- **led** project delivery
- **executed** migration
- **built** GTM strategy

## Quick set
led, built, launched, delivered, drove, owned, implemented, scaled,
optimized, increased / reduced

## Watch-outs
- Don't reuse the same verb twice on a page — vary it.
- Pick the verb that's literally true: "led" implies you owned it,
  "contributed to" is honest when you didn't.
- Past roles use past tense (built, led); your current role can use present
  (build, lead) — but be consistent within each position.
