# Metrics for Engineering Achievements

Numbers make achievements comparable and verifiable. All metrics are for a
specific period. Categories (inspired by Pragmatic Engineer,
https://www.pragmaticengineer.com):

## Money
- Revenue generated (direct and incremental)
- Costs saved

## Business results
- KPIs achieved
- Cases where your work directly or indirectly exceeded the targets

## Reliability
- SLA improved from X to Y
- Number of highest-severity incidents
- Mean time to recovery (TTR) and time to mitigate (TTM); their reduction
- Incidents prevented by proactive work

## Engineering productivity
- Person-hours / days / weeks saved by a project
- Time for a typical developer task reduced by X%

## Code and architecture
- Number of pull requests and code reviews
- Design documents: authored and contributed to (list them)
- Cases where you helped others get something to production

## People
- How many people you onboarded and mentored
- Mentoring outcomes (promotions, professional growth)

## Recruiting
- Interviews conducted
- Hires from cycles you participated in

## Other
- Internal events organized (knowledge sharing, etc.)
- External events organized or participated in where you helped the
  business achieve a result
