# Power Verbs

The goal: every sentence in your resume starts with an action verb.

## Not like this
- responsible for project delivery
- was involved in migration
- helped with GTM strategy

## Like this
- **led** project delivery
- **executed** migration
- **built** GTM strategy

## Power verb examples
led, built, launched, delivered, drove, owned, implemented, scaled,
optimized, increased / reduced

Extended lists by skill category (management, communication, research,
technical, teaching, financial, creative, helping, clerical) are easy to
find by googling "action verbs list for resumes".
