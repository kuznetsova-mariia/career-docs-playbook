# Algorithm: Resume and LinkedIn with an LLM

## Step 1. Gather the raw material
Describe in free form all your achievements from your time at your current
company (and previous ones, one by one). Don't edit, don't trim — just dump
everything you did. Save it to a separate file.

## Step 2. Study the materials
- Metrics for engineering achievements: `cv-writing/01-achievements-numbers.md`
- Power verbs: `cv-writing/02-power-verbs.md`
- The XYZ formula: `cv-writing/03-xyz-formula.md`

## Step 3. Hand it all to Claude
Send the file with your achievements and say roughly the following:

> Help me write a resume that makes recruiters lose their minds the moment
> they see it and offer me 2x right away. Use power verbs from the list to
> describe achievements, and the XYZ formula. Where appropriate, use the
> engineering achievement examples from the metrics list. Take my list of
> achievements written in my own words as the base. [Your positioning: e.g.,
> "I want to grow toward a senior/lead-level software engineer, focused more
> on technical development"] — my resume must clearly reflect this. Wherever
> something is unclear, ask clarifying questions — don't make things up.
> Read about XYZ here:
> https://www.inc.com/bill-murphy-jr/google-recruiters-say-these-5-resume-tips-included-1-formula-will-improve-your-odds-of-getting-hired-at-google.html

Be sure to replace the positioning block with your own: where you're aiming
and what you're focused on. Without it, the resume will end up "about
everything and nothing."

## Step 4. LinkedIn
After the resume, move on to LinkedIn: `linkedin-writing/01-profile-basics.md`
