# Cover Letter

A cover letter is not motivation ("I'm a good person, hire me") and not a
retelling of the resume. It's an argument for why you solve this company's
pain — and why the hiring manager should open your resume first.

## Structure
1. **Company context, no pathos.** Not "I admire your leadership" — show you
   understand their market, product, and challenges. This alone puts you
   above 80% of candidates.
2. **2–3 ultra-relevant achievements.** Not your whole experience — only what
   answers their need: "Increased MAU by 35% in 6 months", "Cut TTM by 20%".
   The hiring manager should think: "this solves my problem."
3. **Why this company** — without flattery: the industry matches your
   experience, the product plays to your strengths, the mission fits your
   career track.
4. **What you deliver in the first month.** Kills doubts: "In my first month
   I can audit the product funnel and deliver 3 quick wins."
5. **Clear CTA:** "Happy to discuss how I can help you achieve XYZ."

## Best practices
- One page, ~180 words.
- No filler: no "dreamed of working here", no "dynamic team of
  professionals".
- Confident, respectful tone — no pleading.
- Light personalization plus real numbers.
