# LinkedIn

For LinkedIn, use a condensed version of the STAR method
(Situation → Task → Action → Result):
https://www.resume-now.com/job-resources/interviews/star-method

Rules:
1. Include your positioning — where you're aiming. For example: "I want to
   grow toward a senior/lead-level software engineer, focused more on
   technical development."
2. Describe your experience for each job separately, not as one wall of text.
3. Power verbs and the XYZ formula apply the same way as in the resume.
